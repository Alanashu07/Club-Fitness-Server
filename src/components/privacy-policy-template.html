Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Introduction

Welcome to ClubFitness ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ClubFitness mobile application (the "App") and related gym services.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Date of birth, gender, height, and weight
  • Membership plan, payment status, and billing details
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Emergency contact details (optional)

Information collected through use of the App:

  • Workout plans, attendance, class bookings, and check-in history
  • Fitness goals and progress data (for example, body measurements and personal records)
  • Device information such as model, operating system, and app version
  • Notification tokens used to send you alerts
  • Crash reports and usage analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and membership
  • To book classes, track attendance, and manage trainer sessions
  • To personalize workout and progress tracking
  • To process payments and send payment reminders and receipts
  • To send important notices such as membership expiry, schedule changes, and offers you have opted into
  • To improve the App, fix bugs, and keep it secure
  • To comply with legal obligations

Health and Fitness Data

Some information you share, such as weight, body measurements, and fitness goals, may be considered health-related. We use it only to provide fitness tracking and training features. We do not sell your health data or use it for advertising.

Permissions the App May Request

  • Camera / Photos: to set a profile picture or scan a QR code for gym check-in
  • Notifications: to send reminders, class updates, and announcements
  • Location (optional): to confirm gym check-in or find nearby branches
  • Storage: to save invoices or progress images on your device

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers: such as payment gateways, cloud hosting, analytics, and messaging services, who process data on our behalf
  • Gym staff and trainers: limited details needed to manage your membership and training
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections and restricted access, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. After you close your account, we delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep it for legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and update your personal information in the App
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of promotional notifications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions such as location

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. Users under 18 should use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, Firebase, and payment providers. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
